Commit Notch

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

The short version

Commit Notch has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. Nothing about you is sent to the person who made it, or to anyone else.

What stays on your PC

Everything Commit Notch keeps lives in %APPDATA%\Commit Notch: your GitHub sign in, encrypted with Windows DPAPI so only your Windows account can read it, a cache of your last contribution data so the app opens instantly, and your settings. Uninstalling deletes all of it.

Who it talks to

GitHub, using your own sign in, to read your contribution calendar, your recently pushed repositories and your profile name and photo. It asks for the repo and read:user scopes because GitHub only shows private contributions to apps with repo access. Commit Notch only reads; it never writes to GitHub.

Cloudflare's time service (time.cloudflare.com), over NTP, to keep the clock accurate. That request carries no information about you beyond what any network request does.

Google Fonts serves the typeface in the setup window and on this site.

Your control

Sign out from the right-click menu at any time; it deletes your sign in and cache. To revoke access on GitHub's side, open GitHub Settings → Applications.
