What's up there

Today

Your last seven days as seven little graph cells, and how many contributions you've made today.

Your year

Click it and it springs open: the full contribution graph, your latest pushes, the repos you put the most commits into, and the year in four numbers. Private repos count. Scroll or use the arrow keys to flip pages.

World clock

Synced to atomic time over NTP every ten minutes, accurate to milliseconds, with four cities you can change.

Set it up in a minute

  1. Install

    Run Commit-Notch-win.exe. It installs for you alone, no admin rights needed, and starts straight away. Windows may say it protected your PC, because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Sign in

    A setup window opens. Click Continue with GitHub, enter the code it shows in your browser, and approve. Your login stays on your PC, encrypted.

  3. Count your private work

    On GitHub, open your profile settings and turn on Include private contributions on my profile.

Prefer the terminal

If you sign in with the GitHub CLI, Commit Notch finds that login on its own and skips the browser step. Run these in PowerShell before or after installing:

winget install GitHub.cli
gh auth login --web --scopes "repo,read:user"

The second command opens your browser to approve. Then click Use the GitHub CLI in setup and I've signed in, or just restart Commit Notch.

Uninstall

Open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find Commit Notch and choose Uninstall. Control Panel's Programs and Features works too, and so does Uninstall… in the app's right-click menu. Uninstalling quits the app and removes it, its start-at-login entry, your encrypted login, the cache and your settings.

Using the portable zip? Right-click an island, turn off Open at login, choose Sign out of GitHub, quit, then delete the folder. To revoke access on GitHub's side too, open Settings → Applications and revoke Commit Notch.

For IT teams

Install and remove silently, per user:

Commit-Notch-win.exe /S
"%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\commit-notch\Uninstall Commit Notch.exe" /S

Where your data goes

To GitHub's API, with your own token, and to time.cloudflare.com for the time. Nothing else. No accounts, no analytics, no telemetry. Your token is encrypted with Windows DPAPI and never leaves your PC.
